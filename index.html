📜 How to play

The roles

  • Traitors (a secret few) know each other. Each night they choose a Faithful to murder. By day they blend in.
  • Faithfuls (everyone else) must work out who the Traitors are and banish them at the Round Table.
  • The Game Master runs the game and is not a player.
  • Latecomers can join mid-game. Their role is set at random by the app or chosen by the Game Master, and kept secret.

Winning

  • Missions add money to the prize pot.
  • If the game ends with only Faithfuls left, they share the pot.
  • If even one Traitor survives to the end, the Traitors steal it all.

A day in the castle

  1. ☕ Breakfast — discover who was murdered overnight.
  2. ⚔️ Mission — a team challenge. Earn money; win secret 🛡 Shields (immune to murder that night) or the 🗡 Dagger (one double vote).
  3. 🪑 Round Table — debate, then everyone votes secretly on their phone. Votes are revealed one by one. The most-voted player is banished and reveals their role.
  4. 🌙 Night — Traitors meet secretly in the app and nominate a victim. They may instead recruit a Faithful (or issue an ultimatum: join or die).
  5. 🔥 Endgame — when few remain, everyone votes to END or BANISH AGAIN. The game ends only if the vote is unanimous.

Every phone looks the same. Keep your screen private, never show your role, and keep an eye on Game Master messages — they stay on screen until cleared. Lying is allowed — that's the game.
